Milk Ledger – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 31 October 2026

Milk Ledger does not collect, send or share any personal data. The days you mark are stored only on your phone, inside the app's own storage. The app has no account, no ads, no analytics and does not request internet access.

When you tap Share bill, the bill text is handed to the app you choose (for example WhatsApp); that app's own privacy policy then applies.

Uninstalling the app deletes your entries.
