Milk Ledger – Support

Milk Ledger tracks daily milk delivery and works out your monthly bill. It is available for Android and iPhone.

How do I mark a day?

Tap a day once for received, again for not received, and a third time to clear it. Days after today can't be marked yet.

How is the bill worked out?

Received days × the daily rate for each quantity: ₹70 a day for 1 litre and ₹35 a day for ½ litre. The bill at the bottom adds both together.

How do I send the bill to my milk vendor?

Tap Share bill and pick WhatsApp, Messages or any other app. The message lists the days, amounts, total and the dates milk was not delivered.

Where are my entries saved?

Only on your phone. There is no account and nothing is uploaded. Deleting or uninstalling the app deletes your entries. See the privacy policy.
